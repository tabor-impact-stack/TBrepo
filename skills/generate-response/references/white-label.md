# White-label and brand voice rules (summary)

Use this for the general rule. Detailed brand rules live in the creator and advocate skills.

## General rule

Many creator-edit and advocate programs are fully white-labeled or embedded. Customers may not know impact.com exists. For these:
- Remove every mention of impact.com.
- Use the brand's opening line and sign-off.
- Use the brand's terms, for example "Creator" or "Advocate" where the brand requires it.

## Where the detailed rules are

- Walmart Creator is the exception. It runs on the native Media Partner interface, so impact.com references are allowed. Its rules are in the creator-edit-support skill.
- Creator Edit programs (Target Storefront, Meta, Airbnb, and others) are in the creator-edit-support skill, with the brand's Confluence pages listed in its references.
- Advocate programs (Function Health and the APJ advocate brands) are in the advocate-support skill. Advocates have no platform login, so never send them to impact.com.

## If the brand isn't covered

Don't guess its voice. Tell the specialist the brand's voice rules are needed.
